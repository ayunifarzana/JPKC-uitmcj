EVERY MOMENT HAS A STORY.

Some goodbyes are never easy, especially when they mean leaving behind a place filled with beautiful memories and people who have become a part of our journey. JPKC was never just about holding a position, completing responsibilities or organising programmes. It was about the people behind every effort, the laughter during difficult days, the countless discussions, the unexpected challenges, the little moments that turned into unforgettable memories, and the friendships that made everything worthwhile. There were days when we felt exhausted, moments when things did not go as planned, and times when we questioned whether we could make it through. But somehow, we managed to overcome everything together. Through every challenge, we learned, we grew, and we became better versions of ourselves. To everyone who was part of this journey, thank you for the memories, the kindness, the support, the patience and even the little things that you may not realise meant so much. Thank you for making this journey more meaningful than words could ever express. Perhaps one day, we will look back at these photographs and wish we could return to these moments just one more time. We will miss the familiar faces, the random conversations, the laughter, the chaos and even the busy days that once felt ordinary. As this chapter comes to an end, may we never forget that we were once part of something special. Our positions may change, our paths may separate, and life may take us in different directions, but the memories we created together will always have a place in our hearts. Thank you for everything, JPKC. This is not just the end of a term but it is the end of a chapter that we will forever be grateful to have lived. Once JPKC, always a part of JPKC.💜
